Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bulby & Bolt makes animated learning videos for young children and publishes them on YouTube at youtube.com/@BulbyBolt. This policy explains what information we handle. In short: we do not collect personal information from anyone.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no comments, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We do not set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page text uses Google Fonts; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Our YouTube channel

All our videos are marked "made for kids", so YouTube turns off comments and personalised ads on them. When you watch our videos, YouTube, not us, handles your information under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We only see anonymous, combined statistics that YouTube shows every channel owner, such as total views.

Bulby & Bolt Autopilot (our upload tool)

We use a small private tool, "Bulby & Bolt Autopilot", to upload our own videos to our own channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its only user, the channel owner, agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

  • What it accesses: with the owner's permission, the right to upload videos and thumbnails and to read the basic details of the Bulby & Bolt channel (its name and ID), so it can check it is uploading to the right channel.
  • What it stores: one sign-in token, kept only on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers or other users.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page, after which the token stops working and is deleted.

Children

Our videos are for children, but we never knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.
